About Me

RAG Pipelines & Multi-Agent Systems

I'm an AI/ML developer passionate about building intelligent systems that work at the intersection of LLMs and information retrieval. Currently pursuing my B.Tech from Mahindra University (graduating 2025), I combine academic rigor with hands-on production experience.

My expertise lies in designing and implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines that achieve high accuracy and low latency. I've built RAG chatbots processing 13,000+ document chunks with 94.3% retrieval accuracy, engineered multi-agent travel planners with 95%+ task completion, and continuously explore cutting-edge agentic AI patterns.

I work extensively with LangChain, LangGraph, and vector databases like Qdrant. On the backend, I architect FastAPI microservices with PostgreSQL, containerize with Docker, and optimize for production deployments. Every system I build prioritizes efficiency, reliability, and real-world impact.
